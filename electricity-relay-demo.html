How Does a Bulb Circuit Work?
A battery pushes electrons through a wire. The bulb converts electrical energy into light and heat.

Circuit OPEN — Bulb OFF
What Is Happening?

When the circuit is closed, an electric field is established through the conducting path. Electrons already present in the conductor respond to this field, producing the electrical flow that transfers energy to the bulb.

Why copper? Copper is a good conductor because it has mobile conduction electrons that can respond readily to an electric field.

Electron flow vs. Conventional current:
Electrons actually move from the negative terminal (−) to the positive terminal (+).
Conventional current is defined as flowing from + to −. This is a historical convention from before the discovery of the electron.

How Does a Relay Work?
A relay uses a small current to control a larger, separate circuit — using an electromagnetic field.
The dashed secondary wires show that the circuit is open until the relay closes the contact.
